How to read the village

Each robot is one trading bot living in its market's village, and walks to the spot that matches what it is doing. The black board in each village is the last 5 hours of price.

Head light colours

  • Watching price — strolling in the village square
  • Aiming — close to an entry, waiting on the yellow mat
  • In a trade, in profit — on the wooden stage (green ring)
  • In a trade, at a loss — on the wooden stage (red ring)
  • Resting / outside trading hours — by the house

How an order travels

A parcel flies from the bot to the bank at the town hall. Winning trades send coins back; losing trades bring a rain cloud.

Portfolio manager (purple robot, town hall)

Claude reads the market, makes calls and sizes each bot's lots; bigger robots have bigger lots. An automatic guard handles the exits. Every decision is scored against real trades, and the manager hops over to whichever bot it is acting on.
